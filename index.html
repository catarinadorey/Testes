Insights & Performance Analysis

Campaign & Region Efficiency

Campaigns in Michigan (MI) and Pennsylvania (PA) consistently outperform New Jersey (NJ) in efficiency and cost control.

  • Top Performer: In October, MI – Generic Casino reached a 51.61% conversion rate with a $3.98 cost per conversion.
  • NJ Weakness: In September, NJ – Casinos reached $22.55 CPA, while PA and MI remained below $7.00.

Spins & Slots Segment

  • In December, PA – Spins & Slots became the highest spend campaign ($981.32).
  • Performance was inefficient with a $14.65 cost per conversion.

Keyword Performance Drivers

  • "free spins": 339.23 conversions in October with $7.58 CPA. November conversion rate reached 25.95%.
  • "casino bonus": Most consistent keyword and highest spend in December ($644.16).

Quality & Inefficiencies

  • Keywords containing "free spins" and "no deposit" generate LP clicks only, not real FTDs.
  • Inefficient keywords:
    • "casino online" – $19.42 CPA (September)
    • "free casino games" – $68.84 CPA (September)
